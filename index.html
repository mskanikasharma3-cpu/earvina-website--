Frequently Asked Questions

What is Earniva?

Earniva is India's #1 Affiliate Marketing Platform. You can convert any e-commerce product link (from platforms like Flipkart, Myntra, etc.) into a Profit Link and share it with your network to earn real cash/profit on every purchase.

How do I use Earniva?

Simply paste any product link into the Make Link section, share the generated Profit Link, and earn a fixed profit margin when someone shops through your link.

Why is Earniva the best Indian affiliate platform?

It's 100% free, requires no technical knowledge, offers the highest earnings, and allows withdrawal to your bank account with a minimum balance of just ₹10.

How easy is this experience?

The platform is designed to be extremely easy to use. Anyone, from students to housewives and influencers, can start earning instantly without any investment.

Is Earniva 100% Free?

Yes, joining and earning through Earniva is 100% free. There are no registration or annual fees whatsoever.

How do I transfer money to my bank?

Once your confirmed profit reaches ₹10 or more, you can easily request a transfer directly to your bank account via NEFT/IMPS from the Earnings page.

How can I become an affiliate of 200+ Brands?

By simply signing up on Earniva, you automatically gain access to affiliate programs for over 200 brands, including Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, and many more, without individual applications.

Who can make money from home using Earniva?

Anyone who has a social network (WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, etc.) or an audience (Students, Homemakers, Influencers, Bloggers) can earn money from home.

How do we help homemakers earn from home?

Homemakers can share deals on household items, groceries, and fashion within their family and friend groups, earning profit on their daily shopping.

How do students earn money on the go?

Students can share deals on electronics, fashion, and books in their college groups and earn easy pocket money without disturbing their studies.
